## 2. Kenyan Timber & Board Yard Measurement Economics

In the Nairobi timber and board market (Gikomba, Kirinyaga Road, Industrial Area Enterprise Rd), melamine MDF and chipboard sheets are **never purchased in arbitrary square meters**. They follow the standard imperial dimension of **8 ft x 4 ft (2440 mm x 1220 mm)** and are sold in discrete commercial fractions:

| Commercial Board Fraction | Dimensions (mm) | Dimensions (ft) | Market Purchase Price (18mm Melamine) | Primary Usage in Portfolio |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quarter Board (1/4 Sheet)** | **1220 x 610 mm** | 4 ft x 2 ft | **KSh 1,050 – 1,200** | Stepped Nightstands (`#03`, `#08`, `#21`), C-Side Tables (`#10`), Stools/Stands (`#25`) |
| **Half Board (1/2 Sheet - Long)** | **2440 x 610 mm** | 8 ft x 2 ft | **KSh 2,000 – 2,200** | 120cm Nordic TV Console (`#01`), 120cm Low Teak TV Console (`#07`), 120cm Open Console (`#30`) |
| **Half Board (1/2 Sheet - Square)** | **1220 x 1220 mm** | 4 ft x 4 ft | **KSh 2,000 – 2,200** | 4-Tier Bookshelf Tower (`#06`), Pedestal Coffee Table (`#16`), Square Coffee Tables (`#15`, `#20`) |
| **Full Board (1/1 Sheet)** | **2440 x 1220 mm** | 8 ft x 4 ft | **KSh 3,800 – 4,200** | Large Multi-Tier Wrapped Coffee Tables (`#18`) |

### Board Space Optimization & Off-cut Economics
1. **Quarter-Board Yielding**: Small pieces like Bedside Tables (`#03`, `#08`, `#21`) fit entirely within a single **1/4 Board (1220 x 610 mm)** with **>80% panel utilization**. Purchasing only a quarter board saves **KSh 2,750** per build compared to buying a full board.
2. **Half-Board Yielding**: 120cm TV consoles (`#01`, `#07`, `#30`) use decks of 1200x380mm. By having the board yard rip an 8x4 sheet lengthwise into two **2440 x 610 mm** halves, the entire console is fabricated from one half, preserving the other half for the next incoming order.
3. **Off-Cut Preservation**: Contrasting accent drawer faces (such as the Warm Oak drawer fronts on the Nordic TV console `#01`) utilize quarter-board off-cut strips (600x300mm), which board yards sell at substantial discounts (**KSh 400 – 600**).
